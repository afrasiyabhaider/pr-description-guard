## Icon Description

The extension features a **golden shield icon with a checklist design**:
- **Shield Shape:** Represents protection and guarding
- **Golden Yellow Color:** Matches the warning theme (#F0C36D)
- **Three Horizontal Lines:** Represent the three required sections (What, Why, Tested)
- **White Checkmark:** Indicates validation and quality assurance
- **Platform Agnostic:** The design works for all Git platforms (GitHub, GitLab, Bitbucket, etc.)

### Screenshot Specifications
- **Size:** 1280x800 or 640x400 pixels
- **Format:** PNG or JPEG
- **Content:** Must show the extension in action
- **Quantity:** At least 1, up to 5 screenshots
